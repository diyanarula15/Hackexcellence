## Country outlines

`countries.json` contains only Japan, United Arab Emirates, Thailand,
Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Vietnam from Natural Earth 1:50m
Admin-0 Countries, redistributed as TopoJSON by
[world-atlas 2.0.2](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas). Natural Earth
data is public domain; see the [Natural Earth data terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
and [50m countries source](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries/).
